## Starting Position

1. Follow the machine's instructions and begin at an easy level.
2. Stand upright with your feet supported on the steps. Use the rails for balance.

## Movement

1. Step at a controlled pace that allows stable footing.
2. Reduce the level before stepping off. Use the machine's stop control.

## Key Points

- Record elapsed duration and machine steps when known. Resistance levels belong to that machine.
- No lifting load, repetitions or strength PR is assigned to this activity.
