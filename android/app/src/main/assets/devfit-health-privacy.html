DevFit and Health Connect

DevFit is a personal workout tracker based on the open-source Liftosaur project.

Your permissions

Health Connect access is optional. You control each permission in Android settings and can revoke access at any time.

With your permission, DevFit reads bodyweight, body fat, sleep, calories and protein. DevFit can write bodyweight, body fat, completed workouts and active calories to Health Connect.

Your data

DevFit stores your training and imported health data on your device. This build disables automatic Liftosaur cloud synchronization, analytics and crash reporting.

You can export your training database from Me → Import / Export. Keep exported files somewhere you trust. Uninstalling DevFit deletes its local database.
